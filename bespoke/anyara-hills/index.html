01 — The land

Older than everything we will build on it

The estate sits against Banjaran Titiwangsa, the range that runs the length of the peninsula and carries the third-oldest rainforest on earth. Elevation gives it weather the valley does not get: cool mornings, mist to mid-morning, and a natural hot spring on site.

Twenty-five minutes from the city, and far enough that you cannot hear it. The masterplan is drawn to keep the ridgeline unbuilt — every lot looks at the range, not at another house.

02 — Masterplan

Twenty-six lots, one ridgeline

03 — Security

Privacy, engineered

The reason a buyer chooses a gated estate over a bungalow lot they could buy anywhere. It deserves more than one line in a paragraph.

01

Twelve-foot double perimeter wall

Two walls with a monitored dead zone between them, following the full estate boundary. Not a fence with a gate.

02

Over 200 cameras

Full perimeter and internal road coverage, with recorded retention and a manned control room on site.

03

Drone patrol

Scheduled aerial sweeps across 584 acres, covering ground that vehicle patrols cannot reach quickly.

04

Armed guards and single controlled entry

One gatehouse, screened access, registered vehicles. Every arrival is expected before it happens.
